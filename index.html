About

I started as a medical officer at a Basic Health Unit and moved step by step into management — district health offices, a tehsil hospital as Medical Superintendent, and district-level maternal, newborn and child health programmes.

Since May 2026 I have been Administrator of a 100-bed gynaecology and paediatrics hospital in Jhang, with ICU and NICU, run by a charitable trust. I joined on day one of its revamp: opening OPD, then emergency, theatres and wards, and then building the systems that let a new hospital report, comply and improve.

What I care about: making the next person's job easier. If you learned something the hard way, it is on you to make it easier for whoever comes next. That is how systems, professions and nations grow.

“It's not a building problem. The real work is getting people to use the system every day — and leaders to look at the numbers.”— a lesson from the hospital revamp

Data & reporting

Daily stats to a live dashboard

Paper daily reports moved into one data log, per-metric pages and a management dashboard, with rules for holidays and working days so trends stay honest.

Patient experience

Complaints & grievance system

A policy, locked response timelines, escalation levels, positive-feedback logging and monthly department reviews.

Quality

PHC / MSDS compliance

Committee, focal persons for each functional area, a phased workplan and weekly progress reporting to leadership.

People

HR policy & merit pay framework

An experience-points matrix, merit levels, a service-commitment scheme for critical-care staff, and a staff handbook in English and Urdu.

Finance

Costing & source tie-outs

Step-down cost per patient, phased cost-recovery targets, and a rule that every figure is cross-checked against at least one other source.

Front desk

Billing at the counter

A mobile web form that puts each department's money beside its patient count, with a reviewer check — reconciliation built in at entry.
