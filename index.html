  1. Administrator

    Private / Trust
    Red Crescent Hospital, Jhang (Sheikh Sharif Memorial Trust) May 2026 to now

    Helped open a 100-bed hospital for mothers and children, and now run it.

    Key achievements
    • 15 working systems feeding one dashboard: daily stats, complaints, HR, quality, pharmacy stock, equipment
    • Complaints left with no action fell from 42% to 0%
    • 10-phase PHC MSDS plan with a committee and 21 focal persons
    • Pharmacy and hospital registration with PHC in progress
    • HR policy that is strict on performance and fair on basic needs
    • Building an A-to-Z reporting system for higher management

    What I learned: In government you fill in the charts. Here every decision and outcome is yours. An institution can become bigger than any one person's name when the right SOPs are in place.

    • Oversaw the outsourcing of Maryam Nawaz Health Clinics

    What I learned: How to bring in a big change across a whole district.

  3. DHA

    District Coordinator, IRMNCH & Nutrition Program

    District
    District Health Authority, Faisalabad April 2023 to December 2025

    Led mother, newborn and child health services across Faisalabad district.

    Key achievements
    • Managed the outsourcing of 170+ dispensaries, BHUs and RHCs
    • Ran 30+ Clinic on Wheels vehicles in 5 tehsils over two phases
    • ANC visits, nutrition screening, family planning, LHW field work and timely referrals
    • Effective surveillance of the RAS ambulance system; monthly reports counter-checked
    • Orientation sessions and monthly meetings with LHVs and LHSs on every indicator
    • Trained all LHVs on Implanon NXT and new contraceptive methods
    • Rationalised HR to keep 24/7 centres fully functional
    • Improved office records, main store and medicine supply chain
    • Trained new health managers and newly hired staff
    • Free Model Health Mela 2025 with partner NGOs
    • Worked with UNICEF, JICA, WFP, Qatar Charity, Greenstar/TCI, BNP and the Population Welfare Department; supported the district's graduation from The Challenge Initiative

    What I learned: The versatility of this role opened my eyes to administration. When staff understand an indicator, they work to improve it.
