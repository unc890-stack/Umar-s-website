Engagement

How assignments are structured.

Advisory

Ongoing counsel to boards, owners and senior management on decisions, risks and performance.

Assessment

An independent review of a facility or programme with a prioritised, costed plan.

Implementation

Design and installation of systems, with the staff who will run them trained and accountable.

Interim leadership

Hands-on administration for openings, transitions and turnarounds, through to handover.

Approach

On site, measured, handed over.

Dr. Nawaz takes on demanding assignments — facilities that must open on schedule, programmes with hard targets, services that need to be turned around — and stays with them until the systems run without him.

  1. Diagnose on site. Records, registers and every department, before any recommendation.
  2. Agree the plan. Options and a clear recommendation, decided with leadership.
  3. Build with the team. Practical tools tested on live data, not imposed from outside.
  4. Hand over. A named owner, a standard operating procedure and a review cycle for each area.
  5. Measure. Baseline and follow-up figures reported to leadership.
Profile

Dr. Umar Nawaz

Dr. Umar Nawaz is a physician and public-health manager based in Faisalabad, Pakistan. He trained in medicine at the University of Lahore and holds a Master of Public Health, with a focus on epidemiology, maternal and child health and hospital management.

His career has moved deliberately from clinical practice into management, across almost every level of Punjab's health system: primary care, a district headquarters hospital, district health offices, a tehsil hospital as Medical Superintendent, and district coordination of maternal, newborn, child health and nutrition programmes. He has also worked on the public–private side, managing the outsourcing of primary health facilities.

Since May 2026 he has been Administrator of a 100-bed mother and child hospital run by a charitable trust, leading it from first opening through the systems that now govern its daily operations, compliance and finances.

He works with clinicians and managers alike, and is equally at ease with a district programme review and a hospital ward round.
