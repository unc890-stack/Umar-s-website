About me

A doctor who runs health care.

I started as a doctor in a small village clinic. Over time I took on bigger jobs. I ran a town hospital. I led health programs for a whole district. Today I run a 100-bed hospital for mothers and children in Jhang.

I know the clinic floor and the office desk. So I can talk to doctors, nurses and owners in a way they all understand.

  • I have done the work I advise on
  • I have worked for the government and for private hospitals
  • I measure results before and after
  • I keep patient and staff data private
