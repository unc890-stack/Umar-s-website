About

I started with a house job in teaching hospitals and a posting at a rural Basic Health Unit, then moved step by step into management — district health offices, a tehsil hospital as Medical Superintendent, district maternal, newborn, child health and nutrition programmes, and the outsourcing of primary health facilities.

Since May 2026 I have been Administrator of a 100-bed gynaecology and paediatrics hospital in Jhang, with ICU and NICU, run by a charitable trust. I joined on day one of its revamp and have been building the systems that let a new hospital report, comply and improve.

I'm now deepening my skills in health data analytics, because good decisions in health come from good data and well-oriented teams.
