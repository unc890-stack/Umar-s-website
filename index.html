Case study · 2026

Opening and systematising a 100-bed mother & child hospital.

A trust-run hospital with gynaecology, paediatrics, ICU and NICU. I joined as Administrator on day one of its revamp.

~10 weeksOPD → emergency → theatres & wards → full services
15 systemsreporting, dashboard, billing, HR, quality, finance
42% → 0%complaints left without a department action

  • Step-down cost per patient with phased cost-recovery targets
