About me

A doctor who runs health care.

I started as a doctor in a small village clinic. Over time I took on bigger jobs. I ran a town hospital. I led health programs for a whole district. Today I run a 100-bed hospital for mothers and children in Jhang.

I know the clinic floor and the office desk. So I can talk to doctors, nurses and owners in a way they all understand.

  • I have done the work I advise on
  • I have worked for the government and for private hospitals
  • I measure results before and after
  • I keep patient and staff data private
My work

Ten years, step by step.

Each job taught me something new about how health care really works.

2026 to now

Administrator

Red Crescent Hospital, Jhang

I helped open a 100-bed hospital for mothers and children, and now I run it.

  • Set up 15 working systems for daily reporting, complaints, HR, quality and pharmacy stock, all in one dashboard.
  • Complaints left with no action fell from 42% to 0%.
  • Customized the HMIS so it is easy for clinical staff and gives an accurate picture of clinical care.
  • Wrote SOPs and policies that meet national and international standards and fit ground realities.
  • Started a 10-phase plan to meet PHC Minimum Service Delivery Standards (MSDS). A committee and 21 focal persons carry it out, with a weekly progress report.
What I learned: building a system is easy. Getting people to use it every day is the real work.
2025

District Manager

Punjab Health Facilities Management Company, Faisalabad

I helped private partners take over and run government clinics.

  • Evaluated stock and equipment of each health facility before outsourcing.
  • Oversaw the outsourcing of Maryam Nawaz Health Clinics.
What I learned: how to bring in a big change across a whole district.
2023 to 2025

District Coordinator, IRMNCH and Nutrition Program

District Health Authority, Faisalabad

Our district was last in Punjab on every service indicator for months. I set out to change that.

  • Held regular review meetings and explained each indicator to LHVs, LHSs and LHWs.
  • Improved the data quality of EMR and of daily, weekly and monthly reports.
  • Led the district to graduation from The Challenge Initiative (TCI) Family Planning program.
  • Managed the outsourcing of 170+ Dispensaries, BHUs and RHCs.
  • Managed 30+ Clinic on Wheels (COW) vehicles in 5 tehsils over two phases, and helped launch Maryam Nawaz Health Clinics.
  • Worked with UNICEF, JICA, TCI and other NGOs on Nutrition and Family Planning goals.
  • Ran the Free Model Health Mela 2025 with partner NGOs: Hepatitis B and C screening, diabetes, nutrition and Family Planning.
What I learned: when staff understand an indicator, they work to improve it.
2023 to 2024

Medical Superintendent

THQ Hospital, Chak Jhumra

I ran the town hospital while also leading the district program.

  • Met the Universal Health Insurance (UHI) targets.
  • Increased the number of beds with limited HR.
  • Maintained the supply chain and used the budget well.
  • Started implementing PHC Minimum Service Delivery Standards (MSDS).
What I learned: with few staff and a fixed budget, good planning makes the difference.
2022 to 2023

Deputy District Health Officer

District Health Authority, Faisalabad

I led service delivery at all health facilities of the tehsil.

  • Ran EPI, Polio and MR campaigns and Dengue control across 48 UCs.
  • Worked to meet campaign targets and the indicators of each health facility.
  • Implemented EMR and EPI SOPs.
  • Led case response to notifiable diseases.
  • Managed the budget of all health facilities in the tehsil, including BHUs and RHCs.
  • Supervised Polio teams in the field and the revamp of an RHC.
What I learned: field visits show what reports do not.
2020 to 2022

Medical Officer

DDHO Office, Sangla Hill, Nankana Sahib

I supported tehsil health administration and service monitoring.

  • Also held the extra charge of District Surveillance Coordinator.
  • Also held the extra charge of Deputy District Officer Health, Nankana Sahib.
